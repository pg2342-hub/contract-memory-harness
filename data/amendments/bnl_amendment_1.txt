AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO OUTSOURCING AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (the "Amendment") to the Outsourcing Agreement effective May 1, 2006 (the "Agreement") between Virtual Item Processing Systems, Inc. ("VIP") and Brokers National Life Assurance Company ("BNL") is entered into as of January 15, 2008.

1. Minimum Fee. The minimum monthly fee payable by BNL is increased from five thousand dollars ($5,000) per month to six thousand five hundred dollars ($6,500) per month (the "Minimum Fee"), effective February 1, 2008.

2. Governing Law. The governing law section of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following: "This Agreement and performance hereunder shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas without regard to conflict of laws."

3. No Other Changes. Except as set out in this Amendment, the Agreement remains in full force and effect. If this Amendment conflicts with the Agreement, this Amendment controls.
