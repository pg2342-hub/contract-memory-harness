AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO LICENSE AND HOSTING AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (the "Amendment") to the License and Hosting Agreement dated October 29, 1999 (the "Agreement") between Corio Inc. ("Corio") and Commerce One, Inc. ("Commerce One") is entered into as of March 1, 2001.

1. Term. Section 11.1 of the Agreement is amended so that the initial term of the Agreement is seven (7) years from the Effective Date, instead of five (5) years. All references in the Agreement to the "initial five (5) year term" now refer to the initial seven (7) year term.

2. Renewal. After the initial seven (7) year term, the Agreement continues to renew automatically for one (1) year periods on the same notice terms as before.

3. No Other Changes. Except as set out in this Amendment, the Agreement remains in full force and effect. If this Amendment conflicts with the Agreement, this Amendment controls.
